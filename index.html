How it works

Four steps, then it just runs in the background.

Allow location

ShopPing finds the real stores near you — the big chains where you live, plus anywhere else you shop.

Pick your stores

Choose the ones you actually go to, then build a list for each — name, photo, even a link.

Set your distance

One default radius for everything, or fine-tune it store by store — in kilometres or miles, whichever your part of the world uses.

Walk within range

Your phone alerts you right there — with exactly what's still on that store's list.

Good to know

  • Nearby search shows the closest few, not every branch. "Find nearby stores" lists up to the 5 closest within 50 miles (80 km), and "Any location" alerts cover up to the 8 closest. In a city with seventy Walgreens you'll see the nearest handful.
  • Store further away, or a chain that isn't listed? Add it with "+ My own store" and put the pin on the map yourself — it gets its own list and alerts, like any other store.
  • Searching by name looks around the pin. When adding your own store, move the pin to the right area first, then search — otherwise it looks near wherever the pin already is.

Under the hood

The small settings that make it actually usable.

Every list app has an add-item button. Here's what ShopPing adds once you've used it for a week.

Distance, per store

Your grocery store might get a half-mile heads-up; the pharmacy you only remember at the door can ping you at 50 feet.

"Any branch" mode

Turn on any-location alerts for a chain and it fires near whichever branch you pass — not just the one you first picked.

Stores that aren't chains

Drop a pin for the corner shop or the farmers' market stand — it gets its own list and alerts, just like the big names.

Text size, your call

Four steps up from the default, one tap back down — no hunting through your phone's system settings.

Sort, or just drag

A-Z, most items first, or drag stores and items into whatever order matches how you actually shop.

Checked items step aside

Tick something off and it slides down to its own "already bought" section instead of cluttering what's left.

Send a list to someone

Hand the shopping to whoever's passing the shop. It arrives as a message; what's already on their list stays exactly as it was.

Six languages

English, Russian, French, German, Spanish and Portuguese — it follows your phone, or you can pick one yourself.

Photo or link, per item

Snap a picture of the exact one you want, or paste a product link — handy for anything that's easy to mix up in the aisle.

No accounts, no server

Nothing about your list leaves your phone.

ShopPing was built to keep your data off servers on purpose. There's nothing to sign up for, nothing to sync, and no shopping-list data sitting on a server anywhere that could leak.

  • Your list is stored only on your device — not in an account, not in the cloud.
  • Alerts are calculated on your phone — no location is sent anywhere to trigger one. (Finding a store's address in the first place does use Apple Maps, see below.)
  • No ad network, no analytics dashboard, nothing to sell.
  • A list only ever leaves your phone if you send it yourself — and it travels inside the link, not through us.
