Privacy Policy

ShopPing ("the App," "we," "us") is developed by Natalia Gurevich. This Privacy Policy explains what information the App interacts with and how it's used. We've written it in plain language wherever possible.

The short version: ShopPing has no user accounts, no server, and sends no data anywhere. Everything you enter stays on your device.

1 Information we collect

The App works entirely offline, without any account or sign-up. The following is created and stored only on your device, in the App's local storage:

  • The store chains you select.
  • Your shopping list items — names, checked/unchecked status, notes, and any photos or product links you add.
  • Store locations, if you use the App's testing feature to assign a location to a store.
  • Your chosen alert-distance settings, both the overall default and any per-store overrides.

We do not collect your name, email address, phone number, or any other account information — the App has no accounts to collect them for.

2 Location data

With your permission, the App uses your device's location to calculate the distance between you and stores you've saved, so it can show a local notification when you're nearby. This calculation happens entirely on your device. Your location is never sent to us or stored on any server we control.

To find real store locations near you, the App sends the coordinates you provide (your current location or a dropped map pin) to Apple's Maps service — this is required for the search to work, and is the only way your location leaves your device. It is not sent to us, not stored beyond that single search, and not used for advertising or analytics.

You can revoke location permission at any time in your device's Settings. The nearby-store notification feature will stop working, but the rest of the App will continue to work normally.

3 Camera & photos

If you attach a photo to a shopping list item, the App will ask for access to your camera or photo library. Photos you select are stored locally as part of your shopping list data. We never upload, view, or have access to these photos.

4 Data storage & security

All App data lives locally on your device using standard iOS/Android app storage. It isn't protected by a separate password, so anyone with access to your unlocked device could view it — we recommend relying on your device's own screen lock.

Because there is no server, there is no copy of your data anywhere else, which also means we cannot recover it if you lose your device, uninstall the App, or clear its storage.

5 Data sharing

We do not sell, rent, share, or transmit your data to any third party, advertiser, or analytics provider — the App sends no data anywhere. It currently includes no third-party SDKs, analytics, or advertising services.

If you tap a product link you've added to an item, you'll leave the App and open that page in your browser; that site's own privacy practices apply, not ours.

6 Your choices

Because everything is stored locally, you're always in full control:

  • Delete individual items or stores at any time within the App.
  • Uninstalling the App permanently deletes all of its data from your device (subject to your device's own backup behavior, such as iCloud or Google device backups, which we don't control).
  • Revoke location, camera, or notification permissions at any time in your device's system Settings.

7 Children's privacy

The App is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from children — nor from anyone, since everything stays on-device. The App is intended for a general audience aged 13 and older.

8 Future changes to how data is handled

If we add features that require a server, an account, or data leaving your device — for example, syncing your list across your own devices — we will update this Privacy Policy first and clearly describe what changes and what choices you have.

9 Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The effective date at the top will reflect the latest revision. Continuing to use the App after changes take effect means you accept the revised policy.
